Habits that grow an audience

  • Open with a clear promise in the first line.
  • Reply to every comment in the first hour.
  • Pin your best post and put one link in your bio.
  • Collaborate with accounts that share your audience.
  • Post a short video each week, since video reaches the most new people.
  • Follow platform rules for giveaways, including disclosure.
